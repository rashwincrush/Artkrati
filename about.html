Creator of ideas, swimming in the sparkling sea✨ of imagination

At AAKRATI Interior Design Artist, we believe that interiors are more than just spaces—they are stories waiting to be told, emotions waiting to be felt, and reflections of the souls who inhabit them.
Four years ago, after over a decade of experience working with renowned design firms, I decided to start my own journey driven by a vision to create interiors that are not just beautiful, but deeply personal and meaningful.
Nestled in the vibrant heart of Bengaluru, we craft residential, commercial, and hospitality interiors that seamlessly blend timeless elegance with contemporary flair. From bespoke furniture to curated décor, textures, lighting and materials, we weave together artistry and functionality.
Guided by craftsmanship, sustainability, and an eye for detail, we create interiors that inspire, comfort, and endure—reflecting the uniqueness of those who live or work in them.
